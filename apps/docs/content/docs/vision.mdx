A loyalty and guest relationship tool for hotels in Tunisia. The hotel owns the relationship with the guest, not the booking site or the tour operator.

## Principles

1. **Own the relationship, not the booking.** The hotel keeps the contact, whoever sold the room.
2. **Capture first.** No contact means nothing to automate. See [capture first](/docs/decisions/capture-first).
3. **Local channels.** WhatsApp and email first, SMS later, in French, Arabic and English.
4. **Configuration, not customization.** Branding is config, never code per client. See [branding is config](/docs/decisions/branding-is-config).
5. **Feedback leads to action leads to revenue.** A survey that nobody reads is noise.
6. **Recognition over points.** See [recognition over points](/docs/decisions/recognition-over-points).
7. **Long term: a destination network** across hotels, restaurants and spas.

## Segments

The buyer is the owner or the general manager.

| Order | Segment | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guesthouses and boutique hotels up to 30 rooms | First. The owner decides fast. |
| 2 | Hotels with a large Tunisian clientele | Locals are reachable and rebook. |
| 3 | Spa and restaurant hotels | Offers have a clear place to land. |
| 4 | Tour operator all-inclusive resorts | Later. They do not own their guests. |
| 5 | Groups and chains | Later. |

Which segment the first three pilots come from is still an [open decision](/docs/decisions#open-decisions).

## Why a hotel says yes, in order of speed

| When | What the hotel gets |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Unhappy guest alerts. The manager fixes it the same day. |
| Month 1 | More Google reviews, because every guest gets the invite. |
| Months 2 to 3 | A contact list the hotel owns, and offers to fill low season, the spa and the restaurant. |
| Month 6 plus | Direct rebooking saves the tour operator or booking site commission. |

The biggest gain is the slowest to show, so the pitch opens with the fastest one. See the [pitch playbook](/docs/pitch).

## Why a guest scans

A small perk for scanning, recognition on the next visit and on their birthday, a real person who reads the feedback, and offers that fit instead of spam.
