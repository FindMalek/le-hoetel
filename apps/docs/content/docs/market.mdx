## Tunisia market

- Over 11 million visitors in 2025. 2026 is slightly down, about minus 0.5 percent from January to August.
- Occupancy was 35.3 percent in H1 2025 on 173,970 beds. 164 to 177 hotels are closed.
- Tour operators squeeze prices and margins. Tunisians are over 45 percent of Hammamet reservations in one report.
- Guest reviews complain about dated rooms, bland all-inclusive food, extras charged, and managers dismissing complaints.
- Xlia (Arabsoft) is a PMS that runs in 400 plus hotels. Its API is unknown (to verify).

What this means for us: most hotels live on tour operator packages and probably never see the guest contact, so capture comes first. Low occupancy makes offers to fill low season a real need. Reviews that mention ignored complaints make the unhappy guest alert a strong opener.

## Channels and rules

| Channel | What we know |
|---|---|
| SMS | One way only. Three operators. A sender ID must be registered. An operator API needs a direct contract. |
| WhatsApp | Billed per delivered template since July 2025. The Tunisia rate is unknown (to verify). Template approval takes time, so we submit templates in week 1. |
| Email | Booking sites mask guest emails, so we capture the real one directly. |
| Social | Facebook has about 9.2 million users in Tunisia and Instagram about 4.3 million. |

### Law and data

- Law 2004-63 and the INPDP apply.
- A draft law mentions fines up to 200,000 dinars. Its status is unknown.
- Hosting guest data abroad may need a legal check. A lawyer checks consent wording and hosting before the first pilot.
- This is our reading of the law, not legal advice.

The rules we apply are in [data and consent rules](/docs/data-rules).

### Payments

Local payments include D17 and e-DINAR. Stripe may not work for Tunisia (to verify). Billing is manual at first, see [pricing](/docs/pricing).

## How the research connects to decisions

| Finding | Decision |
|---|---|
| Hotels may hold no contacts and the Xlia API is unknown | [Capture first](/docs/decisions/capture-first), no PMS connector in v1 |
| SMS is one way and sender ID is slow | [Email and WhatsApp first](/docs/decisions/email-and-whatsapp-first) |
| Tour operators own their guests | [Small local hotels first](/docs/decisions/small-local-hotels-first) |
| Guests say programs lost value | [Recognition over points](/docs/decisions/recognition-over-points) |
| Prices are untested | [Free pilot then paid](/docs/decisions/free-pilot-then-paid) |
