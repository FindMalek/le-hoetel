---
title: Risks
description: What can go wrong and how we reduce each risk.
---

| Risk | Fix |
|---|---|
| Guests and staff ignore the QR | Pilot in 3 hotels, QR in rooms and on tables, small perk |
| No PMS integration, staff type twice | CSV import now, connector later |
| WhatsApp cost and template approval | Prepaid balance, submit templates in week 1 |
| SMS rules and sender ID | Do the paperwork during V1, ship SMS later |
| Data law and hosting abroad | Lawyer checks consent wording and hosting before the first pilot |
| Hotels will not pay | Free pilot and price talks, adjust plans |
| Custom work per hotel | Branding config only, everything else on the roadmap |
| Tour operator hotels do not care | Not our first segment |

## The big one: capture

If guests get nothing for their phone number, the QR gets ignored. Everything else depends on capture, so the pilot measures it first: 200 consented guests per hotel in month one.
