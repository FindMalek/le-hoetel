Ritorno is a loyalty and guest relationship tool for hotels in Tunisia. A guest scans a QR code in the room or at the restaurant table, leaves a real contact and a clear consent, and the hotel can then talk to that guest on WhatsApp and email in French, Arabic and English: welcome, birthday, offers, and a short survey after the stay. The hotel owns the relationship with the guest, not the booking site or the tour operator.

This site is the single source of truth. Everything we decided, and why, lives here.

</Callout>

## The product in one flow

1. A guest scans the hotel's QR code.
2. They enter name, phone, language and tick consent separately for WhatsApp and for email. The consent is logged.
3. The hotel now owns a real contact.
4. Messages go out on their own: welcome, birthday, holidays, offers.
5. 24 hours after checkout a short survey runs. A low score alerts the manager, who fixes it the same day. Every guest gets the Google review invite.
6. The guest books direct again.

## What is in the repo

- `apps/web`: the landing page and the public guest pages. It only talks to the api
- `apps/dashboard`: login and the hotel and admin app. It only talks to the api
- `apps/api`: orpc on bun. The only app that touches the database, auth, storage and providers
- `apps/docs`: this site
- `packages/*`: shared code, see [structure](/docs/structure)
- `docs/plan`: the original planning board, see [the plan](/docs/plan)
