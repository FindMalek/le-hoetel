Ritorno keeps your hotel's own list of guests: who agreed to hear from you, what you offered them and what they thought of their stay. This guide is for the people who use it every day. It is written in English only. The app itself is in English, French and Arabic, and the button names below are the English ones.

## The idea in one minute

1. A guest scans a QR code in the room, on a table or at the desk, or you add them yourself.
2. They give their name and phone number and say yes or no to WhatsApp and to email, each on its own.
3. Ritorno sends the welcome, birthday and holiday messages, and asks how the stay went.
4. A low score reaches you the same day, so you can put it right.
5. At the desk, the guest shows an offer code and you mark it used.

## Three rules for everyone

- Only tick a consent the guest really gave. Guests can say stop at any time and that always wins.
- Never write down passport or ID numbers or payment cards, not even in a note.
- Guest details stay in Ritorno. Do not copy them to a personal phone list or a chat group.

## Who can do what

Your account has one role per hotel. A menu entry you cannot see is not part of your role.

| Task | Staff | Manager | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Add and edit guests, record a visit | yes | yes | yes |
| Give, send and redeem offers | yes | yes | yes |
| Follow up unhappy guests, see messages and metrics | yes | yes | yes |
| Create offers and campaigns, holiday greetings | no | yes | yes |
| Import a guest file | no | yes | yes |
| Hotel settings, activity log, WhatsApp balance | no | yes | yes |
| Invite and remove colleagues | no | yes | yes |
| Export or delete guest data | no | yes | yes |

Owners also receive the weekly summary, and cannot be removed from the team page.
