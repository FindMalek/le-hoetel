## Plans (TND per hotel per month)

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 99 | Up to 15 rooms, 1,000 guests, email and WhatsApp, welcome, birthday, survey |
| Growth | 249 | 16 to 60 rooms, 5,000 guests, campaigns, offers with redemption QR, low score alerts |
| Pro | 549 | 60 plus rooms or spa and restaurant, unlimited guests, custom sender domain, SMS later, priority support |

## Around the plans

- Setup fee: 300 to 600 TND, free for the first three pilots.
- WhatsApp and SMS: cost plus 20 to 30 percent, drawn from a prepaid balance, so there are no surprise bills.
- Pilots are free for 60 to 90 days, then paid.
- Annual prepay gets two months free.

## Anchor

Western hotel CRMs ask about 5 to 15 USD per room per month. At about 3 TND per USD (assumption) that is 15 to 45 TND per room, so 249 TND for 40 rooms (about 6 TND per room) sits well below it. See [competitors](/docs/competitors).

## How we test it

Ryan tests the plans in 5 to 10 hotel talks and asks:

- Per room or flat price
- Bank transfer, cash or D17
- Which plan the hotel would pick, and what would make them pick the next one

Use the [price-test sheet](/docs/pitch#price-test-sheet) to record the answers.

## Billing

- Billing is manual at first.
- We must pick the legal entity that invoices hotels before the first paid month. This is an [open decision](/docs/decisions#open-decisions).
- Stripe may not work for Tunisia (to verify). Local options are D17 and e-DINAR.

See also [free pilot then paid](/docs/decisions/free-pilot-then-paid).
