title: WhatsApp templates
description: The exact texts to submit to Meta for approval, in en, fr and ar.
---

Business-initiated WhatsApp messages must use a template that Meta approved. Each message type is one template name with three translations. The dispatcher (`sendToGuest`) picks the name from the message type and the language from the guest, and fills the named variables. Hotel name is the hotel's display name; `guest_name` is the guest's name.

Submit them all with `pnpm --filter @le-hoetel/notification whatsapp:setup` (needs `KAPSO_API_KEY` and `WHATSAPP_BUSINESS_ACCOUNT_ID`). The texts live in `packages/notification/src/whatsapp-guest.ts`; a test keeps this page in sync with them.

## Rules for every template

- Category: Marketing. Variables are named, in lowercase with underscores.
- The body never starts or ends with a variable, and a variable never holds a line break.
- Each template has the same footer: the STOP line. Guests who reply STOP are unsubscribed, see [Messaging](/docs/messaging).
- Nothing is sent without the guest's WhatsApp consent. A missing offer or link fails the message instead of sending an empty variable.
- Delivery status comes back on `POST /whatsapp/webhook` (Kapso, signature checked) and updates the `messages` row.

## Welcome: `guest_welcome`

Variables: `guest_name` (example: Amel), `hotel_name` (example: Dar Amel).

**English (`en`)**

Body:

```text
Hello {{guest_name}}, welcome to {{hotel_name}}. We are glad to have you with us and we are here if you need anything during your stay.
```

Footer: `Reply STOP to unsubscribe.`

**French (`fr`)**

Body:

```text
Bonjour {{guest_name}}, bienvenue à {{hotel_name}}. Nous sommes ravis de vous accueillir et restons à votre disposition pendant votre séjour.
```

Footer: `Répondez STOP pour vous désabonner.`

**Arabic (`ar`)**

Body:

```text
مرحبًا {{guest_name}}، أهلًا بك في {{hotel_name}}. يسعدنا استقبالك ونحن في خدمتك طوال مدة إقامتك.
```

Footer: `للإلغاء أرسل STOP.`
