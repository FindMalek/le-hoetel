---
title: Free pilot, then paid
description: The first three hotels pilot free for 60 to 90 days. After that they pay if they see results.
---

**Status:** accepted, November 2026

## Context

Our prices are untested guesses, and we have no proof yet.

## Decision

Ryan runs a free pilot in three hotels and price talks with 5 to 10 hotels at the same time. Plans are adjusted from what we learn.

## Why

- Prices are untested, so we learn from real hotels.
- Ryan can sell with proof.
- Building for months before selling risks building the wrong thing.

## Why not

- Building for months before selling.

## Consequences

- Pilot hotels get a free service, so success measures must be agreed up front.
- Billing is manual at first and the invoicing entity is still open.

## Revisit when

Two paying hotels, or hotels refusing to pay after a successful pilot.
