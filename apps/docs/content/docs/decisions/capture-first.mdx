---
title: Capture first, no PMS connector in V1
description: Start with a QR page and CSV import. Skip a PMS connector in V1.
---

**Status:** accepted, November 2026

## Context

Hotels are the source of every guest contact, but many hold none. Tour operator and booking site guests arrive with masked or missing contacts. A PMS connector looks attractive because it would sync guests automatically.

## Decision

V1 captures guests with a public QR page, staff add and CSV import. There is no PMS connector.

## Why

- Hotels may hold no contacts at all, so there would be nothing to sync.
- The Xlia API is unknown (to verify), and Xlia runs in 400 plus hotels.
- PMS integration is the top competitor complaint: it breaks and support is slow.
- No contact means nothing to automate, so capture has to come first.

## Why not

- PMS connector in V1: slow to build, depends on vendors we cannot reach yet, and does not fix hotels with no contacts.

## Consequences

- Staff may type twice if the hotel also uses a PMS. CSV import covers the gap for now.
- The data model stays open so a connector can sync guests in V3.

## Revisit when

Xlia confirms an API, or pilots show capture works and staff double entry is the main blocker.
