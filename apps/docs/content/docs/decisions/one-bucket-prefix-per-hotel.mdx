---
title: One bucket with a prefix per hotel
description: All hotels share one S3 compatible bucket. Each hotel's files live under its own prefix.
---

**Status:** accepted, November 2026

## Context

Hotels have logos, QR posters and exports that need storage.

## Decision

One bucket, a folder prefix per hotel, and access always resolved from the hotel id.

## Why

- Less operations work: one bucket to back up, monitor and secure.
- A bucket per client adds cost and setup for no gain unless a hotel asks for data separation.

## Why not

- A bucket per client: stronger separation, more to run.

## Consequences

- Every storage call must be scoped by hotel id. Isolation is enforced in code, not by the bucket.
- We can move one hotel into its own bucket later if it asks.

## Revisit when

A hotel requires physical data separation.
