---
title: Recognition and simple perks, no points or tiers
description: Recognise guests and give simple perks through offers. No points, tiers or currency.
---

**Status:** accepted, November 2026

## Context

Loyalty programs are the default idea of what a hotel should run.

## Decision

We build recognition on the next visit and on the birthday, plus simple perks delivered as offers with a redemption QR. No points, tiers or currency.

## Why

- About half of loyalty members say hotel programs lost value (Accenture 2025).
- 89 percent want to choose their rewards.
- Independent, simple programs are gaining.
- It is much less to build and to explain at reception.

## Why not

- Points and tiers: heavy to build, hard to explain, and guests already distrust them.

## Consequences

- A recognition wallet is possible later (V3), still without points.
- Perks must be real, otherwise the QR gets ignored.

## Revisit when

Pilot hotels ask for points, or guests stop responding to perks.
