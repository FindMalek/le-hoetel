---
title: Small local hotels first
description: The first pilots are guesthouses, boutique hotels and hotels with a large Tunisian clientele. Tour operator resorts come later.
---

**Status:** accepted, November 2026

## Context

The market has everything from guesthouses to all-inclusive resorts with very different buyers.

## Decision

We start with hotels up to 30 rooms and hotels with many local guests. Which segment the first three pilots come from is still an open decision.

## Why

- The owner decides fast.
- Local guests are reachable and rebook.
- Tour operator resorts do not own their guests, so loyalty matters less to them.

## Why not

- Tour operator resorts first: a long sales cycle and they do not own the guest relationship.

## Consequences

- The first wins are smaller in revenue per hotel. Groups and chains stay for later.

## Revisit when

Pilots in small hotels succeed and a destination cluster appears.
