---
title: Guests are not auth users
description: Guests are rows owned by a hotel. They do not sign up and they do not have a login.
---

**Status:** accepted, November 2026

## Context

Guests give a contact and a consent in under a minute. A login would stop most of them.

## Decision

A guest is a hotel-scoped record with consent entries. Guest actions such as unsubscribe, export and delete work without a login.

## Why

- Any extra step lowers capture, which is our biggest risk.
- Guests belong to a hotel, not to our platform: the same person at two hotels is two relationships.
- Auth users are for staff and admins only, which keeps the auth surface small.

## Why not

- Guests as better-auth users: friction, and it mixes staff and guest identities.

## Consequences

- Guest actions (unsubscribe, export, delete) need a link or request flow that does not depend on a session.
- A guest web app in V3 will need its own identity design.

## Revisit when

V3 guest web app or the destination network needs a guest account.
