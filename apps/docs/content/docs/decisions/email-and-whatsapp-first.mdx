title: Email and WhatsApp first, SMS later
description: V1 sends email and WhatsApp. SMS comes in V2.
---

**Status:** accepted, November 2026

## Context

Hotels in Tunisia reach guests through several channels, and SMS is the obvious one.

## Decision

V1 ships email and WhatsApp template messages. SMS comes in V2, and the sender ID paperwork starts during V1.

## Why

- SMS is one way only, there are three operators, and the sender ID registration is slow.
- An operator API needs a direct contract.
- WhatsApp is where Tunisians already talk.
- SMS lifts survey replies 30 to 50 percent (vendor claim), so it stays on the roadmap.

## Why not

- SMS on day one: slow paperwork would delay the first pilot.

## Consequences

- WhatsApp is billed per delivered template since July 2025 and the Tunisia rate is unknown (to verify). We use a prepaid balance.
- WhatsApp template approval takes time, so templates are submitted in week 1.

## Revisit when

The SMS paperwork completes early, or pilots show guests do not answer on WhatsApp or email.
