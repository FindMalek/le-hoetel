## Hotel guest CRM vendors

| Vendor | What we know |
|---|---|
| Revinate | 12,500 plus hotels and about 900 million guest profiles (vendor claim) |
| Cendyn | Guest marketing and CRM |
| Cloudbeds Guest Marketing | About 300 to 1,800 USD a month for 24 to 100 rooms |
| SendSquared | Claims 1,000 to 5,000 USD a month for small hotels (vendor claim) |
| GuestMaker | Guest marketing |
| D-EDGE CRM | Hotel CRM |
| Directful | Guest marketing |
| Hapi Guest | About 5 USD per room per year |

The typical range is 5 to 15 USD per room per month.

## Common complaints

- PMS integration breaks
- Slow support
- Opaque pricing
- Extra fees for reports

These are why we start with capture, publish clear plans, and do not depend on a PMS connector in v1. See [capture first](/docs/decisions/capture-first).

## Open source CRMs

Twenty, EspoCRM, Mautic and Listmonk. None of them is hotel specific.

## White label and local

- No white label hotel CRM exists. Agencies resell GoHighLevel instead.
- No Tunisian loyalty competitor found. An adjacent tool is RevWay (to verify).

## Where we can win

- Price: local plans in TND, not USD per room.
- Local channels: WhatsApp and email in French, Arabic and English.
- No PMS needed to start.
- Recognition and simple perks instead of a points scheme.

Whether a local competitor exists is still open, see [what we could not confirm](/docs/not-confirmed).
