## V1 in detail

V1 is what we build now. In order:

1. Hotels as organizations with branding config and a super admin panel. One bucket with a prefix per hotel.
2. Guest, consent and consent log tables, scoped by hotel.
3. Public QR capture page, mobile first, branded, in French, Arabic and English, with separate consent checkboxes. Staff add and CSV import.
4. Unsubscribe, privacy pages, guest export and delete.
5. Email with Resend and react-email. Jobs: welcome, birthday, holiday calendar, one-off campaigns to simple segments.
6. WhatsApp via Kapso template messages. Approval takes time, so templates are prepared in week 1.
7. Offers with title, text, validity, promo code and a redemption QR.
8. Post-stay survey 24 hours after checkout, six questions at most, low score alert, Google review invite for everyone.
9. Hotel dashboard and the feedback widget.
10. Production deploy and pilot onboarding.

**Not in V1:** SMS, PMS integration, points or tiers, AI features, guest app, custom domains, WhatsApp inbox, multi-property, online payments, booking engine.

**Success for V1:** three pilot hotels, 200 consented guests each in month one, at least one hotel still using it, two paying by the end of month three.

## V2: reach and trust

SMS with a registered sender ID, custom sender domains, segment builder with saved segments, WhatsApp team inbox for reception, guest profiles, Google and Tripadvisor review flow, Xlia CSV mapping, multi-property accounts, Arabic dashboard and printable QR kit, local billing.

## V3: connect and learn

PMS connectors (Xlia first), stay-based journeys, online pre check-in that captures real emails of booking site guests, AI summaries and campaign drafts in French and Arabic (a human approves), offer suggestions, recognition wallet, guest web app, anonymous benchmarks.

## V4: destination network

Shared loyalty across hotels, restaurants and spas in a destination, guest app, AI concierge, direct booking requests with local payments, group analytics, open API, Maghreb expansion after a data law check.

## The long game

1. Start with capture and messages. No integration needed.
2. Connect their CRM or PMS and sync guests into ours.
3. Take over one module at a time: guests, then campaigns, then surveys.
4. Add what they cannot get elsewhere: AI, offers, recognition.
5. Their old system is no longer needed.

Replacing a PMS is huge, because hotels run rooms and billing on it. Each step must be useful alone. Do not build any of this early, but keep the data model open for it.

## Exit plan (our reading)

A buyer pays for recurring revenue, low churn, hotels clustered in one destination, integrations like Xlia, clean data agreements and anonymous benchmarks. A raw guest database is a liability, not an asset.
