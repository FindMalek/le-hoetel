## What guests think

- About half of loyalty members say hotel programs lost value (Accenture 2025).
- 89 percent want to choose their rewards.
- Spam is the top complaint, at 44 percent (older survey).
- Upgrades are the top perk, at 47 percent.
- Independent, simple programs are gaining.

## Booking sites and direct guests

- Booking sites mask emails, and the masked address expires after the stay.
- Direct guests returned at 5.1 percent versus 1.3 percent for booking site guests (one vendor, take with salt).

## Surveys

Best practice we follow:

- NPS style, six questions at most
- Sent within 24 hours of checkout
- SMS lifts replies 30 to 50 percent (vendor claim), which is one reason SMS comes in a later version
- Never gate reviews by score: everyone gets the Google review invite

## What this means for the product

| Insight | Product choice |
|---|---|
| Programs lost value | Recognition and simple perks, no points. See [recognition over points](/docs/decisions/recognition-over-points). |
| Guests want to choose | Offers that fit, a few perks guests can pick |
| Spam is the top complaint | Send less, segment, unsubscribe or STOP on every message. A CSV import never re-subscribes a guest who opted out |
| Booking sites hide emails | Capture the real contact on site with the QR page |
| Replies need low effort | Short survey, mobile first |
