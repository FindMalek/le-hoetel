## The guest journey on the board

1. A guest stays at the hotel.
2. They scan the QR code in the room or at the restaurant table.
3. They enter name, phone, birthday and tick yes for WhatsApp and email. The consent is logged.
4. The hotel now owns a real contact.
5. Messages go out on their own: welcome, birthday, holidays, offers.
6. A survey runs 24 hours after checkout. A low score alerts the manager. A high score gets a Google review invite, and everyone is asked, never only the happy ones.
7. The guest books direct again.
