Research was done in November 2026. These items could not be confirmed. Until they are, treat every related statement elsewhere in the docs as a hypothesis.

| Question | Why it matters | Status | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tunisia WhatsApp rate on the Meta rate card | Sets the cost of WhatsApp messages and the markup in [pricing](/docs/pricing) | Unknown | Check the Meta rate card and a test template |
| Does Xlia have an API, and who to ask | Decides the PMS connector in V3 and the Xlia CSV mapping in V2 | Unknown | Ask hotels that run Xlia and Arabsoft |
| Status of the new data law, and whether INPDP approval is needed to store guest data | Gates the first pilot and hosting | Unknown | Legal check before the first pilot |
| Whether a local loyalty competitor exists | Changes positioning. RevWay is an adjacent tool (to verify) | Not found | Ask hotels and check local directories |
| What hotels will actually pay | All prices are guesses | Untested | 5 to 10 price talks, see the [price-test sheet](/docs/pitch#price-test-sheet) |
| Whether Stripe works for Tunisia | Decides how we bill | To verify | Check, plan manual billing with D17 or e-DINAR |
| Which legal entity invoices hotels | Needed before the first paid month | Open | Decide with the team |

## Research gaps

Some documentation pages of the template modules (jobs, logger, organizations, notifications, feedback, storage) could not be opened during research. Check them against the source when building each module.

## Vendor claims

These figures come from vendors and are not independently confirmed:

- Revinate: 12,500 plus hotels and about 900 million guest profiles
- SendSquared: 1,000 to 5,000 USD a month for small hotels
- SMS lifts survey replies 30 to 50 percent
- Direct guests returned 5.1 percent versus 1.3 percent for booking site guests (one vendor)
